UGI Corporation (NYSE: UGI) is a King of Prussia, Pennsylvania-based natural gas and electric utility and propane distribution company. UGI Utilities serves Pennsylvania natural gas and electric customers, AmeriGas is the largest retail propane distributor in the United States, and UGI International markets LPG across Europe through brands including Antargaz, Flaga, and AvantiGas. The corporation also operates midstream and energy marketing businesses. This repository captures the APIs, developer tools, and machine-readable API artifacts for UGI Corporation.

## APIs

No public developer portal, OpenAPI specs, or third-party developer APIs have been identified for UGI Corporation. Customer self-service runs on a Smart Energy Water (SEW)-powered Online Account Center (Android package `com.sew.ugi`) with CheckFree Pay handling bill payments. AmeriGas operates the separate MyAmeriGas portal at myamerigas.com. Large commercial and industrial customers use an Oracle APEX-based TCP platform at apex.ugi.com. UGI Utilities is not listed as a supported utility by UtilityAPI or other Green Button aggregators at the time of profiling. Contributions welcome.
